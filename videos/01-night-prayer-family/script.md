# Night Prayer for Your Family's Protection: script

Voice: calm and slow, at about 145 wpm. Music: soft piano or pad at -24 dB under the voice, no drums.
Visual base: a looped night scene (a house with one lit window under stars, rain on pine trees, or a
calm moonlit ocean). Captions are word-by-word, centred low, and are the only motion besides the loop.

---

## BEAT 1: Hook (0:00–0:15)

[ON SCREEN: The lit window glows slowly. Caption: "Pray this before you sleep."]

Before you close your eyes tonight, pray this over your husband, your wife and your children.
But first, there is one thing you must stop carrying to bed.

## BEAT 2: The turn (0:15–0:45)

[ON SCREEN: The camera drifts slowly toward the window. Caption: "Psalm 4:8"]

In the next few minutes, we will place every person under your roof into the hands of God,
one by one. Then we will lay down the thing you have been carrying. And we will pray for one
more person at the very end.

The word of God says in Psalm chapter 4, verse 8:
"I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, LORD, only makest me dwell in safety."

Only God. Not your watchfulness. Not how late you stay awake. Let us pray.

## BEAT 3: Coming before God

[ON SCREEN: Full scene, no camera movement. Caption: "Heavenly Father, I come to You tonight."]

Heavenly Father, I come to You at the end of this day.
I come as I am: tired, a little worried, and grateful that You are still awake when I am not.

Thank You for the breath in my lungs today.
Thank You for the people You have placed in my home.
Thank You for every meal, every conversation, every small mercy I did not stop to notice.

Lord, I confess that I have not always trusted You with the people I love.
I have tried to hold them together with my own hands.
I have worried as if worry could protect them.
I have stayed awake as if my watching could keep them safe.

Forgive me, Father. Teach me again that You are the keeper of my house.

Your word says in Psalm 121:
"He will not suffer thy foot to be moved: he that keepeth thee will not slumber.
Behold, he that keepeth Israel shall neither slumber nor sleep."

You do not sleep, Lord. So tonight, I can.

## BEAT 4: My husband or wife

[ON SCREEN: The scene dissolves to two cups on a quiet table. Caption: "For my husband. For my wife."]

Father, I lift up my husband, my wife, the one You gave me to walk beside.

You know the weight they carry that they do not say out loud.
You know the pressure at work, the worries about money, the tiredness behind their eyes.

Protect their body as they sleep.
Protect their mind from fear and discouragement.
Protect our marriage from distance, from bitterness, from words spoken in anger that we cannot take back.

Where we have hurt each other, bring honesty and repentance.
Where we have grown quiet, bring tenderness back.
Where trust has worn thin, rebuild it slowly, on truth.

Let our home be a place of peace, not a place of tension.
And when tomorrow comes, let us choose each other again.

If you are not married tonight, or you have lost the one you loved, Lord, be their companion.
Fill the empty side of the room with Your presence.

## BEAT 5: My children

[ON SCREEN: The scene dissolves to a child's bedroom lit by a nightlight, curtains moving gently.
Caption: "For my children."]

Lord Jesus, I bring You my children.

The little ones asleep down the hall.
The grown ones living far away.
The ones who call me every week, and the ones who have not called in a long time.

You know each of them by name. Right now, in the quiet, I say their names to You.

[PAUSE: 4 seconds, music only. Caption: "Say their names to God."]

Father, guard their bodies. Keep them safe on the roads, at school, at work, and at night.
Guard their minds from lies, from shame, from voices that tell them they are not enough.
Guard their hearts from friendships that pull them away from what is good.

For the child who is struggling, give strength.
For the child who is lost, draw them home, not by my pressure, but by Your love.
For the child who is sick, give healing, good doctors, and patience in the waiting.
For the child who feels alone, send one faithful friend.

I cannot follow them everywhere, Lord. But You can.
Your word says in Psalm 121, verse 8:
"The LORD shall preserve thy going out and thy coming in from this time forth, and even for evermore."

Their going out. Their coming in. Tonight and every night.

## BEAT 6: Parents, family and the people I love

[ON SCREEN: The scene dissolves to a porch light on an old house in the rain. Caption: "For my family."]

Father, I lift up my parents, my brothers and sisters, and the family I do not see often enough.

For those who are growing older, give comfort, health, and dignity.
For those who are far away, keep them close to You.
For those we are not speaking to, soften what has hardened, and show me my part in making peace.

And for the people I love who do not know You yet, Lord, open the door gently.
Let them see Your kindness in me before they hear it from me.

As for me and my house, Lord, we will serve You.

## BEAT 7: Protecting the home

[ON SCREEN: Back to the house with the lit window, now under heavier stars. Caption: "Watch over this house."]

Heavenly Father, watch over this house tonight.

Over every door and every window.
Over every room where someone I love is sleeping.

Keep us from harm, from accident, from fire, from danger we cannot see.
Keep fear from coming in through the news, through the phone, through the thoughts that come at 2 a.m.

Let Your peace fill this home like light fills a room.
Not because these words have power of their own, but because You are faithful, and You hear the prayers of Your children.

## BEAT 7b: Psalm 91 read over the home

[ON SCREEN: A wide shot of the house, with rain on the pines. Each verse number fades in at the
top left. Caption: "Psalm 91"]

Let us rest now in the words of Psalm 91, spoken over this house and everyone in it.
Read it slowly. Let each verse settle.

He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty.
I will say of the LORD, He is my refuge and my fortress: my God; in him will I trust.
Surely he shall deliver thee from the snare of the fowler, and from the noisome pestilence.
He shall cover thee with his feathers, and under his wings shalt thou trust: his truth shall be thy shield and buckler.
Thou shalt not be afraid for the terror by night; nor for the arrow that flieth by day;
Nor for the pestilence that walketh in darkness; nor for the destruction that wasteth at noonday.
A thousand shall fall at thy side, and ten thousand at thy right hand; but it shall not come nigh thee.
Only with thine eyes shalt thou behold and see the reward of the wicked.
Because thou hast made the LORD, which is my refuge, even the most High, thy habitation;
There shall no evil befall thee, neither shall any plague come nigh thy dwelling.
For he shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee in all thy ways.
They shall bear thee up in their hands, lest thou dash thy foot against a stone.
Thou shalt tread upon the lion and adder: the young lion and the dragon shalt thou trample under feet.
Because he hath set his love upon me, therefore will I deliver him: I will set him on high, because he hath known my name.
He shall call upon me, and I will answer him: I will be with him in trouble; I will deliver him, and honour him.
With long life will I satisfy him, and shew him my salvation.

"I will be with him in trouble." Not "there will be no trouble," Lord, but You will be with us in it.
That is the promise we hold tonight.

## BEAT 8: The thing you must stop carrying to bed. THE PAYOFF

[ON SCREEN: Slow fade to the open sky over the water. Caption: "Lay it down."]

Now, I promised you at the start that there was one thing you must stop carrying to bed.

It is this: the belief that keeping your family safe is all on you.

You have been carrying it for a long time. You check the phone one more time. You run through the
worst things that could happen. You lie awake as if your worry were a wall around the people you love.

But worry is not a wall. It is only a weight.

Jesus said in Matthew chapter 11, verse 28:
"Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest."

And the apostle Peter wrote:
"Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you."

So if you are able, open your hands where you are lying, and speak these words with me.

[ON SCREEN: The caption shows each line on its own, one line at a time.]

Lord, I cannot keep my family safe by worrying.
I place my husband, my wife, my children into Your hands.
I place my parents and the people I love into Your hands.
I place this house into Your hands.
I place tomorrow into Your hands.
You are awake, so I can rest.
Not my will, Lord, but Yours be done.

[PAUSE: 3 seconds, music only.]

That is the thing you lay down tonight. You do not have to pick it up again in the morning.

## BEAT 9: The one person we pray for last

[ON SCREEN: A single candle on a windowsill, the night outside. Caption: "And one more person."]

And I told you there was one person in your home we would pray for last.

It is you.

You pray for everyone else. But who prays for you?

Heavenly Father, I lift up the one who is listening right now.
The one who has been strong for everyone else and is tired tonight.
The one who smiles at the table and cries in the car.
The one who has prayed for years and is still waiting.

Meet them with Your comfort.
Give their body real rest tonight. Deep sleep, without fear, without bad dreams.
Give their mind quiet. Let the conversations they keep replaying go still.
Remind them that their worth is not measured by how much they hold together.

Your word says in Proverbs chapter 3, verse 24:
"When thou liest down, thou shalt not be afraid: yea, thou shalt lie down, and thy sleep shall be sweet."

Let that be true tonight, Lord. For this one.

## BEAT 10: Blessing and close

[ON SCREEN: Back to the house. The lit window dims slowly to a soft glow. Caption: "The Lord bless thee, and keep thee."]

Now receive this blessing from Numbers chapter 6:

"The LORD bless thee, and keep thee:
The LORD make his face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee:
The LORD lift up his countenance upon thee, and give thee peace."

Peace over your husband. Peace over your wife. Peace over your children.
Peace over this house. And peace over you.

In the mighty name of Jesus Christ, our Saviour and Lord. Amen.

[ON SCREEN: Caption: "Write the names of your family in the comments. We will pray for them."]

If this was the prayer of your heart, write the names of your family in the comments,
and we will pray for them too. Then close your eyes. You can rest now.

## BEAT 11: Music outro

[ON SCREEN: The loop continues, captions off. The music rises slightly, then fades. Use end screen
elements here: the next night prayer and the Psalm 91 reading.]
